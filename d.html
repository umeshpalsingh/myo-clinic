How to create your account

Launch the app or visit the website, then follow the steps below so you can start creating your account.

  1. Click on “Sign Up”
  2. You may add your email address, use Google account or Apple ID to sign in.
  3. Enter your Password to create your account.

Once signed up, you’ll be automatically logged in or asked to log in with your new credentials.

To customize your profile, you click the Profile tab and update your details like:

  1. Fullname
  2. Avatar/Icon
  3. Password

That’s it! You’re ready to use the app or website.

TIP: If you are using an iOS or Apple device, Always sign-up using your personal email, not the Private Relay email from Apple (Hide My Email option). That way you can log in on multiple devices without losing access to the purchased plan.
